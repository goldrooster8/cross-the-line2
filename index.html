How to play:
  • Each round one player (the answerer) secretly chooses a spot (1–5) on the ruler.
  • Other players guess that spot by placing a bar (short/medium/long). Shorter = more points but harder.
  • After all guesses, the true line is revealed. If the line falls inside your bar, you score (long=1, medium=2, short=3).
  • Game ends after 10 rounds. Highest total wins!
